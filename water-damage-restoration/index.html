Water damage restoration is a distinct service from plumbing repair. It addresses the cleanup, drying, and mitigation after water has already affected a structure, flooring, drywall, or contents, separate from fixing whatever caused the water in the first place. If a pipe burst and caused water damage in your Lakewood home, you're likely dealing with both problems at once: the plumbing repair itself, covered by our burst pipe repair team, and the water damage that resulted, covered here.

Fast response matters more with water damage than almost any other home issue, since standing water and elevated moisture create conditions for mold growth within as little as 24 to 48 hours. Our water damage line runs 24/7 specifically because of that timeline.

Not every wet spot requires a full restoration response. A small, quickly-caught spill that's dried within a few hours is very different from water that's been sitting under flooring or inside a wall cavity for a day or more. If you're not sure which situation you're in, describing what happened and how long ago is usually enough for us to advise you honestly over the phone.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Standing Water

Needs extraction as soon as possible to limit how far damage spreads.

Visible Water Stains On Walls Or Ceilings

Can indicate water damage inside a wall cavity even without visible standing water.

Musty Odor

Often the first sign of developing mold growth in an area that got wet and didn't fully dry.

Warped Or Buckling Flooring

A sign that water has been present long enough to affect the flooring material itself.

What To Do While You Wait

  • Stop the water source if it's safe to do so, or shut off the main water valve
  • Turn off electricity to any affected area with outlets or fixtures near standing water
  • Remove or elevate furniture and belongings from wet flooring where possible
  • Photograph the damage before any cleanup begins, useful for an insurance claim
  • Avoid running HVAC systems that could spread moisture or mold spores to unaffected areas
