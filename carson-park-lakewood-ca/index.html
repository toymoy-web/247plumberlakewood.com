The Third Phase Of The 1950s Build

Plumbing Help In Carson Park

Carson Park in Lakewood is a residential tract, and it is a separate place from the city of Carson. City history lists it as the third phase of the postwar development, built in 1952 after Lakewood Park and Lakewood Mutual Homes. If you landed here looking for the city of Carson, our Carson page covers it.

Listing sites place the tract toward the east side of Lakewood near the San Gabriel River trail and Rynerson Park. The tract has no formal boundary on the city's maps, so the closest honest description is east-central Lakewood near those landmarks.

The homes belong to the same early-1950s family as the rest of the original build. That is useful to know, but it does not tell us what is inside any particular wall. We look at your house before drawing conclusions.

Practical Guidance

Simple Checks Before You Call

A running toilet or slow drain is easy to ignore. A quick check can show whether the cause is simple.

Helpful Steps

  • Drop a little food coloring in the tank to see if dye reaches the bowl
  • Listen for hissing or trickling after a flush
  • Check the area around the water heater base for moisture
  • Clean pop-up stoppers and strainers before assuming the pipe is blocked
  • Call if the same drain slows again within a few weeks
