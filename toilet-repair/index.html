Toilet repair is one of the most common calls we get in Lakewood, often tied to fixtures that were installed decades ago and never upgraded. A running toilet alone can waste enough water to show up on a bill, and it's usually a simple, inexpensive fix rather than a sign of a bigger problem.

Many original 1950s-era toilets in the city are still on their first or second fixture, and parts for those older models aren't always something a big-box hardware store stocks. We carry a wider range of flappers, fill valves, and flange hardware specifically because this city's housing stock skews older than most of the surrounding area.

We repair running toilets, cracked bowls, and faulty valves, and install full replacements when a fixture is past the point of being worth repairing. If a recurring clog turns out to be a line issue rather than the toilet itself, our drain cleaning team can check the line the same visit.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Toilet Runs Continuously

Usually a worn flapper or fill valve, an inexpensive and quick fix in most cases.

Cracked Bowl Or Tank

A visible crack, even a small one, can worsen over time and is worth replacing rather than patching.

Frequent Clogging

If one toilet clogs far more often than others in the house, the fixture itself may be the issue, not the line.

Rocking Or Loose Base

Often means a worn wax ring or a loose flange, both fixable without a full replacement.

Toilet Running Or Leaking?

Most repairs are quick, inexpensive, and done the same visit.

Repair Or Replace

Which One Makes Sense

Repair

  • Lower cost for a worn flapper or valve
  • Makes sense for a single specific issue
  • Doesn't address a cracked bowl or tank
  • Best for a fixture that's otherwise sound

Replace

  • Fixes a cracked bowl or tank for good
  • Often more water-efficient than an original fixture
  • Higher upfront cost than a repair
  • Best when the fixture itself is failing, not just a part
