Clogged and slow drains are the second most-called plumbing problem in Lakewood, and the reason usually traces back to the city's original cast-iron drain lines. Homes built in the 1950-53 construction window are now carrying more than seventy years of use through the same pipe material, and root intrusion and scale buildup both get worse with age.

Mature landscaping is common across the city's older neighborhoods, from the tree-lined streets around Mayfair Park to the larger lots in Lakewood Country Club Estates. Tree roots seeking out moisture are one of the most frequent causes of a drain line that clogs in the same spot over and over. A standard cable can clear the immediate blockage, but it usually doesn't remove the roots themselves, which is why the same line tends to back up again within a few months if jetting or a targeted repair isn't part of the fix.

We snake standard clogs and offer hydro jetting for lines that keep clogging in the same spot, a sign of root intrusion or a partial collapse a snake alone won't clear. If the same drain has been slow for months, worth pairing this with a water heater check too, since fixture-side issues sometimes surface together in older homes.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Slow Draining Sinks Or Tubs

Water backing up or draining unusually slowly, often the first sign of a partial clog.

Recurring Clogs In The Same Drain

A clog that keeps coming back in weeks usually means root intrusion, not just debris.

Gurgling Sounds

Air trapped behind a partial blockage often gurgles when another fixture drains.

Sewage Odor

A persistent smell near a drain can mean a vent issue or a deeper line problem worth a camera look.

Snake vs. Hydro Jetting

Which Fits Your Clog

Cable Snaking

  • Faster for a single, simple clog
  • Lower cost per visit
  • Doesn't fully clear root intrusion
  • May need repeating within months

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears roots and buildup along the full line
  • Better for cast-iron lines this age
  • Longer-lasting result
  • Higher upfront cost than snaking
