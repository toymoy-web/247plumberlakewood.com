Tankless water heaters need a different kind of maintenance than a standard tank unit, and Lakewood's moderately hard water makes that maintenance more important here than it would be in an area with softer supply. That holds whether the unit is in a Lakewood Estates garage or a Mayfair utility closet. Mineral scale builds up inside the heat exchanger over time, and a system that isn't descaled periodically loses efficiency and can eventually fail well before its expected lifespan.

We service, repair, and install tankless units, and we recommend a descaling schedule based on your actual water hardness and usage rather than a generic interval. If you're considering switching from a tank unit, we'll walk through the honest trade-offs, upfront cost, ongoing maintenance, and the actual benefit for your household size, rather than assuming a tankless system is automatically the better choice for every home.

A tankless unit that's never been descaled in a hard-water area like Lakewood is one of the more common causes of a service call that could have been avoided. Manufacturers generally recommend annual descaling in moderately hard water conditions, and skipping it for several years often shows up as reduced efficiency well before an outright failure.
