What We Handle In Lakewood

We built this service list around what Lakewood homeowners actually call about, not a generic national list. Most of the city's homes were built in the same three-year window in the early 1950s, so water heater failure, drain clogs, and aging-pipe leaks make up the bulk of what our technicians see day to day. Every job gets an upfront flat price before any work begins, and our emergency line runs 24/7 for anything that can't wait for a scheduled visit.
