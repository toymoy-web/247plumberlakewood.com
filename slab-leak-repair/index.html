Slab leaks show up often in Lakewood because most homes here were built directly on a concrete slab foundation during the same three-year construction window, with copper supply lines running underneath. That holds true from the modest tracts near Lakewood Center to the larger homes in Lakewood Estates off Del Amo Boulevard. Seven decades on, those lines are a common failure point, and a leak under the slab behaves very differently from an exposed-pipe leak.

We locate the leak with electronic detection equipment before recommending any concrete work, since opening the wrong section of slab wastes time and money without fixing anything. Repairs are performed to California's adopted Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standards. If detection instead points to a leak inside the water heater itself, that's a simpler water heater repair, and we'll tell you which one you actually have.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Warm Spot On The Floor

A hot water line leaking under the slab often warms the flooring directly above it.

Sound Of Running Water Under The Floor

A hissing or trickling sound with no visible source nearby.

Cracks In Flooring Or Baseboards

Water pressure under a slab can shift flooring and create small, unexplained cracks over time.

Unexplained Water Bill Increase

One of the most common first signs, especially paired with any of the above.

A warm patch on the floor, especially near the garage or a bathroom, is one of the more specific signs of a hot-water-line slab leak, not just general dampness. So is a leak that seems to be coming up near a concrete driveway or garage edge rather than inside the living space. Either is worth an electronic detection visit before assuming what's underneath.

Repair Options

Spot Repair vs. Reroute

Spot Repair

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Minimal concrete opened
  • Doesn't address other aging line sections
  • Best for a single, isolated leak

Reroute Above Slab

  • Avoids further concrete work
  • Line becomes accessible for future repairs
  • Higher upfront cost than a spot repair
  • Better value if the line has leaked before
