Sewer line repair is one of our highest-volume calls in Lakewood, and the original cast-iron main lines running under most of the city's 1950s homes are the reason why. Seventy years of root intrusion, corrosion, and ground settling eventually catch up with even a well-installed line. A full backup, rather than a simple slow drain, is usually the point where a homeowner first realizes something is genuinely wrong with the main line rather than an individual fixture.

We always confirm the actual problem with a camera inspection before recommending a repair, since a root-clogged line, a cracked section, and a settled belly all need different fixes. Trenchless lining work, where applicable, follows ASTM F1216 standards for cured-in-place pipe rehabilitation. If hydro jetting alone can resolve the issue, we'll say so rather than recommend a repair the line doesn't actually need.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Sewage Backing Up Into The Home

The clearest sign of a main line problem rather than an individual fixture issue, and worth immediate attention.

Multiple Drains Backing Up At Once

When more than one fixture backs up simultaneously, it usually points to the main line.

Gurgling From Toilets Or Drains

Air trapped behind a developing blockage in the main line.

Recurring Clogs Despite Repeated Snaking

Often a sign of a structural issue, root intrusion, a crack, or a settled section, that snaking alone can't fix.

Repair Options

Trenchless vs. Traditional Repair

Traditional Excavation

  • Full access to the damaged section
  • Works for any type of damage
  • More disruptive to landscaping or driveways
  • Typically faster to complete

Trenchless Lining

  • Minimal excavation required
  • Preserves landscaping and hardscape
  • Requires the existing pipe to be a viable host
  • Higher upfront cost, less restoration needed after
