Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Lakewood 24/7 Plumbing ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with 247plumberlakewood.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name and phone number when you call our dispatch line, and standard technical information collected automatically by our web host, such as browser type and pages visited. We do not use contact forms on this site; all inquiries come through phone or email.

How We Use Information

Call information is used to schedule and dispatch plumbing service. We do not sell personal information to third parties. Call recordings, where used for quality and training purposes, are handled by our call-tracking provider under its own applicable terms.

Cookies And Analytics

This site may use standard analytics tools to understand site traffic in aggregate. These tools do not collect personally identifying information beyond standard technical data such as IP address and browser type.

Your Choices

You can decline to provide personal information, though this may limit our ability to schedule service. To request that we delete information we hold about you, contact us at contact@247plumberlakewood.com.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@247plumberlakewood.com or (562) 488-9614.
