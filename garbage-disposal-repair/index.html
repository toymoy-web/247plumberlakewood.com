Garbage disposal problems are a steady source of calls in Lakewood, and original kitchen plumbing from the 1950s is part of the reason, whether the home is near Lakewood Center or further out in Lakewood Park. A lot of homes here have had at least one disposal replacement since the house was built, and the connections and mounting hardware from an older installation don't always match current units cleanly, which is a common source of leaks after a DIY swap.

We repair jams, leaks, and units that hum without spinning, and we install new disposals when a unit is past repair or being upgraded. If a disposal leak turns out to be connected to a broader faucet and sink plumbing issue at the same fixture, we'll check both while we're there.

Disposal units generally have a shorter lifespan than the fixtures around them, typically eight to twelve years depending on usage, so a home this age has often already been through at least one replacement. That earlier installation is frequently where problems start: a unit installed by a previous owner, or during a quick DIY swap, doesn't always get mounted or wired to the same standard a licensed technician would use.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Humming But Not Spinning

Usually a jammed impeller, often fixable without a full replacement.

Leaking From The Bottom

Typically a worn seal, common in units nearing the end of their service life.

Frequent Jamming

Can indicate a worn motor or a mounting issue putting stress on the unit.

Unusual Noise Or Burning Smell

Worth turning off and having checked promptly, this can indicate a motor problem.

Repair Or Replace

Which One Makes Sense

Repair

  • Lower cost for a jam or a worn seal
  • Makes sense for a unit still under half its expected life
  • Doesn't address a motor nearing failure
  • Best for an otherwise sound unit

Replace

  • Resets the full expected lifespan
  • Makes sense once a unit is eight-plus years old
  • Higher upfront cost than a repair
  • Best when jamming or leaking has become frequent
