Neighborhoods Added In The 1960s

Plumbing Help In Eastern Lakewood

Eastern Lakewood is a descriptive name for the part of the city east of the San Gabriel River, not a single tract. The city began annexing these unincorporated neighborhoods in the early to mid 1960s. City history says they were built in the late 1950s and early 1960s without the master planning of the central tracts.

Landmarks include Bloomfield Park, which the city acquired from the county in 1964, and Palms Park, where development began in 1969 and a community center and library opened in 1979. The city also annexed an area near Artesia High School in 1967. Listing sites place Imperial Estates in this part of town.

The city later repaved every residential street in the area in the early 2000s and adopted an improvement strategy for it in 2004. For a plumber, the key point is that these homes were built several years after the central tracts, so the pipe and fixture history can differ.

Practical Guidance

Simple Checks Before You Call

Older fixtures give warnings before they fail. These checks take a few minutes and help you decide when to call.

Helpful Steps

  • Read the date on the water heater label and note the age
  • Check under every sink for slow drips or swollen cabinet bases
  • Watch for brown or rusty water when a tap first runs
  • Test the main shutoff valve once a year so it does not seize
  • Call early if a toilet or drain problem keeps returning
