Most of Lakewood is connected to the city sewer system, since the housing stock here was built as a planned, fully-serviced suburb from the start. Septic systems are the exception rather than the rule in this city, typically found on a small number of older or unusually situated properties rather than being common citywide.

For the properties that do have one, we provide standard septic tank pumping on a scheduled basis, along with an inspection of the tank's general condition while we're there. If a septic issue turns out to be a drain field problem rather than simply needing a pump-out, we'll explain what that means and what the realistic next steps look like.

If you're not sure whether your property is on septic or connected to the city sewer, that's worth confirming before assuming either way. A quick check of your property records, or a sewer camera inspection to see where your drain lines actually run, can settle the question, and we can help determine this during any visit if you're uncertain.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Slow Drains Throughout The House

Can indicate a septic tank nearing capacity, worth checking before it becomes a backup.

Sewage Odor Near The Tank Or Drain Field

Worth prompt attention, since it can indicate the tank needs pumping or has a developing issue.

Standing Water Or Unusually Green Grass Over The Drain Field

A sign the drain field may not be processing wastewater properly.

It's Simply Been Several Years Since The Last Pumping

Regular pumping on a multi-year schedule is standard septic maintenance regardless of visible symptoms.
