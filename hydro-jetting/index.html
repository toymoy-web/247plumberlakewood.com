Hydro jetting is high-pressure water cleaning for drain and sewer lines, and in Lakewood it's most often the answer for a line that keeps clogging in the same spot no matter how many times it's been snaked. Most of the city's homes were built between 1950 and 1953, and the original cast-iron drain lines are now over seventy years old. This is especially common near the older street trees around Lakewood Park and Mayfair. Root intrusion through small cracks and joint gaps is a common cause of a recurring clog that a standard cable can't fully clear.

A snake pushes through a blockage and restores flow, but it doesn't remove the roots or built-up scale lining the pipe. Hydro jetting scours the full diameter of the line, which is why it tends to hold up longer than repeated cabling. If a jetted line's problem turns out to be a structural crack or a sagging section rather than roots or buildup, that's a different fix, and our drain cleaning team will say so rather than jet a line that needs a repair instead.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

Recurring Clog In The Same Drain

The clearest sign that snaking alone isn't solving the underlying cause.

Slow Drainage That Keeps Coming Back

A drain that's fine for a few weeks after service, then slows down again.

Gurgling Or Sewage Odor

Can indicate a partial blockage building up gradually along the line.

A Line Already Snaked Multiple Times

If cabling has been tried more than once without lasting results, jetting addresses the cause rather than just the symptom.

If a line has already been snaked and it clogged again within a matter of weeks, that pattern by itself is a strong signal the real cause is root intrusion or heavy scale buildup rather than a one-time blockage. The same goes for a line that got stuck partway through a snake attempt. Both are worth a hydro jetting visit rather than another round of cabling.
