Water heater repair is the single most-called plumbing service in Lakewood, and that isn't a coincidence. Most of the city's homes were built between 1950 and 1953, which means a large share of the original water heaters, and the replacements installed since, are approaching or past their typical service life at roughly the same time.

Lakewood's water runs moderately hard, and hard water accelerates sediment buildup inside a tank faster than it would in an area with softer supply. That buildup is a common reason a unit that's only ten or twelve years old starts rumbling, heating unevenly, or losing capacity well before the manufacturer's rated lifespan would suggest it should.

We repair both tank and tankless units, diagnose the cause before recommending a fix, and give you a flat price before any work begins. All installation and repair work follows California's adopted Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). If the unit turns out to be leaking rather than simply failing to heat, our leak detection team can trace the source the same visit.

Signs & Symptoms

Signs You Need This Service

No Hot Water

Cold showers or a unit that can't keep up with normal household demand.

Rusty Or Discolored Water

Often signals internal tank corrosion, more common in original 1950s-era units.

Popping Or Rumbling Noises

Sediment buildup rumbling inside the tank as it heats, common with Lakewood's moderately hard water.

Water Around The Base

Moisture pooling near the tank usually means a failing tank or a loose fitting, worth a same-day look.
